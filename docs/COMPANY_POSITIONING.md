# MetaHumotonic company positioning

## Category

MetaHumotonic is an **Agent Operations and Verified Compute** company.

The short promise is:

> AI가 생각하고, 333이 계산하고, 영수증이 증명한다.

The company is not positioned as a generic AI studio, a volunteer-computing clone, or a token project. It sells a governed path from agent decision to bounded execution to independently checkable evidence.

## Brand architecture

- **MetaHumotonic** — company and operating platform at `metahumotonic.com`.
- **MetaHumotonic Control** — agent workflow, policy, approval, and audit SaaS.
- **333 Compute Grid** — consented compute supply and verified work exchange.
- **LakatoTree Verify** — prediction, measurement, and receipt-backed evaluation.
- **MetaHumotonic Book** — canon and research entry at `metahumotonic.com/book/`; it is not product marketing. Existing deep publication URLs remain stable on the same origin.

The canon and product are connected by provenance, not merged by branding. Engineering proposals stay labeled as proposals until the user ratifies a canonical relation.

## Position against Palantir

Do not claim “better than Palantir” until customer evidence supports it. The useful competitive wedge is narrower and testable:

- Palantir organizes enterprise data, logic, permissions, and operational action.
- MetaHumotonic aims to organize agent intent, user-owned compute, verification, and execution receipts.
- The differentiator is a closed loop: **decide → grant → compute → verify → settle → learn**.

The launch claim should be “a verifiable agent-to-compute control loop,” not a replacement for every Palantir workload.

## Customer and first workload

The initial customer is a team running repeated AI or research workflows that can separate sensitive orchestration from public, deterministic batch work.

Good first workloads:

- public corpus hashing, chunking, and deduplication;
- deterministic document transforms;
- Merkle-tree and integrity proof construction;
- reproducible evaluation jobs with exact or bounded-tolerance validators.

Customer secrets, private prompts, proprietary model weights, and arbitrary binaries do not enter the browser grid. They remain in a controlled SaaS or approved native tier.

## Revenue model

1. **Control subscription** — per organization for agent policy, workflow, evidence, and audit controls.
2. **Verified Compute margin** — charge customers per verified work unit and pay or credit contributors only after independent verification.
3. **Private Grid** — annual contract for organization-owned nodes, policy, deployment, and support.

The browser alpha issues no money and no transferable token. The first real reward should be non-transferable MetaHumotonic service credit. Cash payout requires a separate payment, identity, tax, fraud, and legal product gate.

## Supply flywheel

```text
useful SaaS demand
  → signed and bounded jobs
  → explicitly consented 333 capacity
  → independently verified results
  → lower unit cost and contributor credit
  → more useful SaaS demand
```

Traffic is not consent. An agent may discover the offer and propose settings, but only the resource owner or an authorized organization administrator may create a ResourceGrant. Browser compute is foreground-only; background CPU/GPU requires a separately installed native provider with a second approval.

## Proof gates

| Stage | What may be claimed | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Local alpha | consent and revoke controls work locally | zero Worker before grant; termination on revoke, hidden tab, and TTL |
| Verified beta | remote work is checked before credit | signed job envelope, independent verifier, durable receipt, duplicate protection |
| Grid beta | multiple contributors execute real work | peer evidence, disconnect/requeue tests, fencing, abuse controls |
| Commercial | customers buy verified compute | invoices, unit economics, SLA, privacy and legal review |

Do not display contributor earnings, grid capacity, customer savings, or settlement finality until the corresponding receipt exists.

## Operating metrics

- verified work units and verification pass rate;
- cost and gross margin per verified work unit;
- queue time, completion time, and retry rate;
- duplicate, stale-fence, and disputed-result rate;
- consent conversion, revoke latency, and post-revoke assignments (target: zero);
- contributor utilization without scope violations;
- percentage of product claims backed by live receipts.

The north-star metric is **customer-useful verified work delivered within an explicit ResourceGrant**, not raw connected devices or reported CPU time.
